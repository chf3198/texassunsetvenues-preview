About Texas Sunset Venues

Our Story

At Texas Sunset Venues, we believe in the power of a getaway to transform, recharge, and reconnect. Our journey started with a simple idea: to create places where every guest feels at home the moment they walk through the door. We’ve combined our love for travel, our passion for hosting, and our desire to offer something affordable yet special—a home-away-from-home that is beautifully decorated and thoughtfully prepared.

As seasoned travelers ourselves, we understand what makes a stay feel special. Whether it's a quiet morning on the beach or a cozy evening by the river, we’ve designed every detail with you in mind. Curtis and Amber, a husband-and-wife team, handle every aspect of your stay, from the decor to the guest experience, ensuring a seamless and unforgettable getaway.

Our Properties

Each of our properties offers something unique—whether it’s the soothing sound of waves at Sea La Vie or the tranquility of the river at River RV. We choose locations that offer the perfect escape from the everyday, where you can unwind, enjoy breathtaking views, and make lasting memories with your loved ones.

All our homes are fully equipped with every amenity you could need, from fully stocked kitchens to peaceful outdoor spaces for stargazing. Our guests love how easy it is to relax, and with our high standards of comfort and care, you can focus on what matters most: enjoying your time away. But remember, weekends fill up fast, so be sure to book your stay soon!

Why Guests Love Us

At Texas Sunset Venues, we don’t just host stays; we create experiences. Our guests tell us they feel like part of the family from the moment they arrive. From the extra touches, like a fully stocked coffee bar, to the thoughtful communication that makes your stay stress-free, we’re here to ensure your getaway is as memorable as it is effortless.

When you book with us, you’re joining a growing community of travelers who appreciate the beauty of a well-crafted space. We can’t wait for you to join the Texas Sunset Venues family and create memories that last a lifetime. And if you love your stay, share your experience with friends and family or tag us on social media—your stories mean the world to us!
