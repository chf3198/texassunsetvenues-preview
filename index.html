Frequently Asked Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Please reach us at txsunsetvenues@yahoo.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

Airbnb or VRBO - which booking platform should I use?

We list on both Airbnb and VRBO, so you can book on whichever platform you prefer.

No matter which platform you choose, you’ll receive the same high-quality service and thoughtful amenities that Texas Sunset Venues is known for. We personally ensure that every stay meets our exceptional hospitality standards—regardless of where you book.

Early check-in : may I arrive earlier than the scheduled time?

We understand the excitement of starting your getaway early, but our team requires the full time between guests to ensure everything is cleaned, restocked, maintained, and inspected for a five-star experience. If you arrive early, feel free to explore the area, familiarize yourself with nearby attractions, and enjoy the community while we prepare your home-away-from-home. If you absolutely need more time, we recommend booking the night before your arrival to guarantee an early check-in.

Events - Do you allow events?

To maintain the tranquility of our properties and protect the facilities, we do not allow events or parties. Our spaces are designed for peaceful getaways and intimate stays.

However, visitors are welcome as long as the total occupancy does not exceed the property’s limit.

Late check-out : may I request a time later than what is scheduled?

Our turnover process ensures that every home is perfectly prepared for incoming guests, so we require the full time between check-out and check-in to maintain our hospitality standards.

Even if the night after your stay isn’t booked at the moment , it may be reserved at any time due to our instant booking and self-check-in options.

For a more relaxed departure , we recommend booking an additional night to reserve the space for your entire last day. This way, you can check out at any time without feeling rushed.

Linens : what linens do you provide?

We provide luxury bed linens, plush towel sets, beach towels, make-up towels, and dish cloths to ensure a comfortable and convenient stay.

Pets - what are your restrictions and fees?

We welcome pets and do not have a set limit on the number of furry guests. However, we ask that pets be well-behaved, cleaned up after, and respectful of the space and neighbors.

Pet Guidelines:

  • No excessive barking or off-leash wandering.
  • Owners are responsible for cleaning up after their pets.
  • Pets should not cause damage to furniture or property.

Our properties are ideal for pets to explore nature and relax by the water alongside their owners.
